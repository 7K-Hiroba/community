# Maintainer teams

Team membership is controlled privately by the organization owner and is not published here. No personal handles are used for repository ownership.

| Team | Responsibility | A1 state and repository mapping |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hiroba-maintainers` | Organization/profile and cross-community administration | Original team preserved; administrator of `.github` and `community` |
| `platform-security` | Combined platform/security review | Original team preserved; triage review access to both repositories |
| `workflows-maintainers` | Sibling workflows repository only | Original team preserved; no access mapping to these two repositories |
| `community-maintainers` | Community governance, registry, and onboarding | Verified absent and created under A1 on 2026-11-14; maintain on `community` |
| `legal-reviewers` | Contribution, legal, trademark, and CLA-status review | Verified absent and created under A1 on 2026-11-14; triage review access to both repositories |

The two additional teams were the only teams eligible for creation after verification. No individual members were added or removed by this task.

## Application teams

Every active application needs at least one `<app>-maintainers` team with repository maintain rights, CODEOWNERS coverage, and an ownership handoff plan. Planned handles in the registry are not claims that those teams or repositories already exist. Creating a future application team requires that application's approved onboarding work, not this foundation task.

Ownership changes require review and documentation. If an application becomes orphaned, `hiroba-maintainers` coordinates handoff or deprecation; it does not silently reassign identity, dashboard prefixes, or trust status.
